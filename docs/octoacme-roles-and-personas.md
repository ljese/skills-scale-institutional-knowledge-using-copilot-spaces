# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Technical Leads / Architects

### Role Summary
Technical Leads and Architects guide technical direction and ensure solutions fit the system architecture. They work with Developers on implementation and keep Project Managers and Product Managers informed about feasibility, dependencies, and technical risks.

### Responsibilities
- Guide architecture and technical design decisions
- Identify integration, scalability, and maintainability risks
- Review technical approaches and help resolve cross-team dependencies
- Advise Developers and Product Managers on feasibility and trade-offs

### Goals
- Deliver solutions that align with architecture and technical standards
- Reduce technical risk and avoid costly integration issues
- Enable Developers to make consistent, well-informed decisions

### Typical Communication
- Design reviews and architecture decision records
- Technical planning with Developers and Product Managers
- Risk and dependency updates with Project Managers

---

## QA Leads / Test Owners

### Role Summary
QA Leads and Test Owners define how quality and acceptance will be verified. They partner with Developers, Product Managers, and Technical Leads throughout delivery, and coordinate with Release Managers before deployment.

### Responsibilities
- Define test strategy, coverage, and acceptance validation plans
- Coordinate functional, integration, and regression testing
- Track defects and communicate quality risks to the delivery team
- Confirm test results and readiness against acceptance criteria

### Goals
- Find issues early and provide clear evidence of product quality
- Ensure acceptance criteria and release requirements are validated
- Support reliable releases without delaying feedback

### Typical Communication
- Test plans, test results, and defect reports
- Ongoing coordination with Developers during implementation
- Readiness reviews with Product Managers and Release Managers

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Stakeholder / Sponsor Representatives

### Role Summary
Stakeholder or Sponsor Representatives communicate business priorities, provide context, and make or secure decisions within their authority. They work with Product Managers to shape outcomes and with Project Managers to align approvals and milestones.

### Responsibilities
- Share business goals, constraints, and relevant stakeholder needs
- Provide timely input on scope, priorities, and proposed trade-offs
- Approve decisions or deliverables when delegated to do so
- Raise concerns and communicate project impacts to represented groups

### Goals
- Ensure project outcomes address business needs
- Make decisions and approvals with clear context
- Keep represented stakeholders informed and aligned

### Typical Communication
- Initiation and milestone reviews with Project Managers
- Priority and outcome discussions with Product Managers
- Decision and approval updates through project status reports

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Release Managers

### Role Summary
Release Managers coordinate release planning and deployment readiness. They bring together Developers, QA Leads, Support / Operations Representatives, and Project Managers to ensure releases are verified, communicated, and recoverable.

### Responsibilities
- Coordinate release scope, schedule, and deployment activities
- Confirm release prerequisites, including testing and rollback plans
- Track readiness, blockers, and deployment status
- Coordinate release communications with Project Managers and stakeholders

### Goals
- Deliver predictable releases with minimized deployment risk
- Ensure teams understand release timing, ownership, and escalation paths
- Make deployment outcomes and follow-up actions visible

### Typical Communication
- Release plans and readiness checklists
- Deployment coordination with Developers and Support / Operations
- Status updates to Project Managers, QA Leads, and stakeholders

---

## Support / Operations Representatives

### Role Summary
Support / Operations Representatives ensure services can be operated and supported after release. They work with Developers and Technical Leads on operational requirements, and coordinate with QA Leads, Release Managers, and Project Managers on launch readiness and follow-up.

### Responsibilities
- Identify monitoring, alerting, support, and operational requirements
- Review deployment and rollback plans for operational readiness
- Prepare support teams with release notes and known-issue guidance
- Surface incidents and production feedback for triage and follow-up

### Goals
- Maintain service reliability and effective incident response
- Ensure support teams are prepared for changes and releases
- Feed operational learning back into product and delivery plans

### Typical Communication
- Operational readiness and handoff reviews with Release Managers
- Incident and service updates with Developers and Technical Leads
- Post-release feedback and risks shared with Project Managers and Product Managers
